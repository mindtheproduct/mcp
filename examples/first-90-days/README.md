# 🧭 Your First 90 Days

A text adventure where you've just taken over a product team. Every choice you make
gets judged against what Mind the Product has actually published about doing this
well — so it's less "which answer did the AI like" and more "here's what people who
have done this say".

Genuinely useful the week before you start a new role.

## Paste this

```
Run a choose-your-own-adventure simulation for me, using the Mind the Product MCP.

Setup: I've just started as Head of Product at a 60-person B2B SaaS company. The
previous head left abruptly. There's a roadmap nobody believes in, an engineering
team that's lost trust in product, and a CEO with strong opinions.

How it works:
- Play out my first 90 days, week by week, in chunks.
- At each decision point give me 3 or 4 options. Make them all defensible — no
  obviously stupid choices.
- After I choose, search Mind the Product for what's actually been written about
  that situation, and tell me how my choice squares with it. Link the article.
- Consequences should compound. If I ignore engineering in week 2, that should bite
  me in week 7.
- Track: team trust, CEO confidence, and shipped value. Show them after each phase.

At day 90, give me an honest debrief and three articles I should have read first.

Start at day 1.
```

## Make it yours

- **Your actual situation** — replace the setup with the company you're joining.
- **Different role** — first-time PM, founder hiring their first PM, IC moving to
  management.
- **Brutal mode** — "Make the CEO actively undermine me and see how I handle it."
