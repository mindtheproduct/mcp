# Things to build with it

Six small projects, all of them a copy-paste away. You need the Mind the Product MCP
connected to Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT or whatever you use — see the
[main README](../README.md) — and then you just paste the prompt.

No setup, no code, no API keys. The AI does the work; the MCP makes sure it's grounded
in fifteen years of real product writing rather than confidently made up.

| | What it is | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🎲 [PM Trivia Night](pm-trivia) | A quiz game with real sources | Team socials, standup warm-ups |
| 🕵️ [Two Truths and a Lie](two-truths-and-a-lie) | Spot the invented product claim | Finding out what you actually believe |
| 🧭 [Your First 90 Days](first-90-days) | A choose-your-own-adventure sim | New product leaders |
| 🔥 [Roadmap Roast](roadmap-roast) | Paste a roadmap, get it pulled apart | Before you show it to the exec team |
| 🃏 [Flashcard Factory](flashcard-factory) | Builds you a real flashcards app | Learning frameworks properly |
| 📍 [ProductTank Radar](producttank-radar) | Finds your people nearby | Getting out of the building |

## Made something?

Open a PR and add it. A folder, a README, the prompt you used. That's the whole format.
