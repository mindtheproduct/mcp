# 📍 ProductTank Radar

ProductTank runs in 200+ cities. Most people don't know there's one near them.

This finds yours, tells you what it is, and — if you want — drafts the message you'd
send to actually turn up.

## Paste this

```
Use the Mind the Product MCP to find product events near me.

I'm in [your city].

Do this:
- Find upcoming ProductTank meetups, conferences, and workshops near me, or online.
- For each: what it is, when, where, and the link.
- If there's nothing close, tell me the nearest one and whether it's worth the trip.
- Then search Mind the Product for anything written about getting value out of
  product meetups, and give me three specific things to do at my first one.

If you find a good one, draft me a short, non-cringe message introducing myself to
the organiser. I'm a [your role] interested in [your thing].
```

## Make it yours

- **Speaker mode** — "I want to speak at one. Which are looking for speakers, and
  what should I pitch given my background?"
- **Team offsite** — "Which conference should my team of five go to this year, and
  what's the case I'd make to my boss?"
- **Travel** — "I'm in Berlin the week of the 14th. Anything on?"
