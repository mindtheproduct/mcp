# 🕵️ Two Truths and a Lie: Product Edition

Three claims about product management. Two are real things published on Mind the
Product. One the AI made up. Spot the fake.

It's a better test of your instincts than a quiz, because the lie is always *plausible* —
that's the point. Plenty of confident product advice sounds exactly like this.

## Paste this

```
Let's play Two Truths and a Lie, product edition, using the Mind the Product MCP.

Each round:
- Search Mind the Product and find two real claims, opinions, or findings from
  actual articles.
- Invent a third that sounds completely plausible but isn't in the content.
- Show me all three, shuffled and numbered. Don't hint at which is which.
- I'll guess the lie.
- Tell me if I'm right, then link the two real ones and explain briefly why the
  fake one is wrong — or why it's a tempting thing to believe.

Play 5 rounds and keep score. Make the lie genuinely hard.

Round 1, go.
```

## Make it yours

- **Turn it around** — "I'll write the three claims, you guess which one I made up.
  Check the MCP before answering."
- **Hard mode** — "The lie should be something a lot of PMs believe but Mind the
  Product has actually argued against."
- **Topic mode** — "All rounds about metrics."
