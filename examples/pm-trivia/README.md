# 🎲 PM Trivia Night

A product management quiz where the questions come from things people actually wrote,
and every answer tells you where it came from. Surprisingly competitive in a team
setting.

## Paste this

```
Let's play PM Trivia Night, using the Mind the Product MCP.

Rules:
- Ask me 10 multiple-choice questions about product management, one at a time.
- Every question must come from real Mind the Product content — search for it first.
- Four options each. Don't make them all obviously wrong; two should be tempting.
- After I answer, tell me if I got it, then give me one or two sentences of
  explanation and link the article it came from.
- Keep a running score.
- Mix it up: frameworks, discovery, stakeholder management, metrics, pricing, hiring,
  a bit of product history.

At the end, give me a score, a title based on how I did, and three articles to read
based on what I got wrong.

Start with question 1.
```

## Make it yours

- **Team version** — "Ask questions one at a time, I'll read them aloud to a group of
  six. Wait for me to type the group's answer."
- **Hard mode** — "Only ask questions where the answer is counter-intuitive or where
  Mind the Product authors disagree with each other."
- **Themed round** — "All ten questions about pricing."
- **Speed round** — "One-word answers only, no multiple choice."
